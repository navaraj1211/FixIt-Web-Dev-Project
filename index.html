How FixIt Works

A transparent and streamlined process that keeps both users and facility administrators connected.

1

1. Report Problem

Select the category, location, priority level, and describe the facility problem in our clean reporting form.

2

2. Track Progress

Receive a unique tracking ID (e.g. FIX-1001) and monitor real-time updates as maintenance teams get assigned.

3

3. Get It Fixed

Facility administrators mark the ticket as Resolved once repairs are inspected and completed successfully.
