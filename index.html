How FixIt Works

A transparent and efficient 3-step workflow designed to resolve issues faster than ever.

1

1. Submit Report

Describe the issue, pinpoint the location, choose or enter a category, and submit your complaint in seconds.

2

2. Live Tracking

Receive an automatic unique ID (e.g. FIX-1001) and follow your report through Pending, In Progress, and Resolved stages.

3

3. Quick Resolution

Facility administrators review tickets, assign technicians, update statuses, and restore operations seamlessly.
